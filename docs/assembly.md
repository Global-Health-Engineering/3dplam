# Assembly

Three printed parts (base, funnel, filter support) become one manifold.
This page covers putting them together. For printing the parts, see
[`printing.md`](printing.md). For choosing which filter support and funnel
variant to print, see [`../hardware/README.md`](../hardware/README.md).

## Building the manifold

1. **Seat the filter support in the base.** The support locates into the
   base through four bayonet pins. Align the pins, drop the support in and
   twist it to lock.
2. **Place the membrane.** Using disinfected forceps, lay the filter
   membrane gridded-side up on the filter support.
3. **Lock the funnel onto the base.** The funnel's lower rim seats over the
   base's top connection and locks down over the membrane.
4. **Seal the membrane.** The base's top brim carries two raised ridges,
   the "hills." Locking the funnel down clamps the membrane between these
   ridges and the funnel's inner slope. The clamp forms the seal that lets
   the syringe pull a vacuum through the membrane rather than around it.
   The outer of the two ridges leaves the membrane edge free, so the forceps
   can catch it when the membrane is lifted off after filtration.
5. **Connect the syringe.** The 100 mL PVC syringe connects to the base's
   water tunnel. Glue this joint with UHU Hartkunststoff Spezialkleber,
   because the syringe can only pull a vacuum through an airtight joint.
   The joint is made once during assembly and is not meant to be
   disconnected between uses.

The project site has a cross-section render of the membrane clamp
(`site/public/renders/membrane-clamp.png`). The render shows the base,
membrane and funnel in distinct colours.

## Taking it apart

To remove the funnel after a filtration, squeeze the funnel's top rim
inward instead of pulling straight up. Squeezing releases the clamp on the
membrane without dragging the membrane off the filter support.

## Retrofitting a commercial funnel

The base's top connection also accepts commercial Millipore Microfil
funnels, so an existing kit can be used with a printed base.
